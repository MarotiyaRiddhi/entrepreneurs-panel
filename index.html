About the panel

Four operators who have
actually shipped.

Most startup advice is written by people who have never had to make payroll. This panel is the opposite: a fintech founder who built India’s first financial platform for women, a CEO scaling an EV and clean-energy group out of Gujarat, and alumni who traded consulting careers for cap tables of their own.

Expect specifics — how the first cheque was raised, what broke at scale, and what they would tell a final-year student sitting on an idea today.
